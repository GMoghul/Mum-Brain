Privacy policy and help · Last updated 17 October 2026

The short version

  • Your own jobs stay on your phone. They aren't sent anywhere.
  • If you share a family list, those jobs are scrambled (encrypted) on your phone before they're sent, so nobody else, including me, can read them.
  • There are no accounts, no adverts and no tracking. Nothing is sold or shared for marketing.
  • If you send feedback from the app, I receive what you write.

Who runs Mum Brain

Mum Brain, Sorted is made by Rose Moghul, an individual developer in the United Kingdom. I am responsible for your information under UK data protection law (UK GDPR).

Your jobs and settings

Your jobs, lists, projects and settings are saved on your own phone. The app keeps a second copy in its own storage on the phone, so they aren't lost if your phone clears space. They aren't uploaded to me or anyone else.

Backups you save in Settings are files on your phone or wherever you choose to keep them (for example, your Files app or iCloud Drive). I never see them.

If you delete the app, everything it saved on the phone is deleted too, so keep a backup if you might want your jobs back.

Family sharing

If you create or join a family list, the jobs on that list are stored online so everyone in your family sees the same jobs. They're kept on Google Firebase, which provides the app's online storage.

Before a shared job leaves your phone, it's encrypted with a key made from your family code. Only phones that know the code can unscramble it. I can't read your shared jobs, and neither can Google.

Keep your family code private, as anyone who has it can join your list. If you leave the family list, your phone stops syncing with it. To have a shared list deleted completely, contact me with your request.

Feedback you send

The feedback form in Settings sends your answers to me, stored on Google Firebase. That's your answers, your name only if you choose to add it, the time you sent it and the app's version number. It isn't linked to your jobs or your phone.

I use feedback only to improve the app, and I delete it once it's no longer needed for that, and at the latest after 2 years. Leave your name off to stay anonymous.

Alarms and the morning summary

Alarms and the morning summary are scheduled on your phone itself. They don't go through any server, and you can turn them off in the app's Settings or in your phone's Settings › Notifications.

Other services

  • Google Firebase stores shared family lists (encrypted) and feedback. When your phone connects, Google's servers see technical details such as your IP address, as with any internet connection. Google may store data outside the UK, protected by legal safeguards for international transfers.
  • Google Fonts supplies some of the app's fonts, which means your phone may connect to Google to download them.
  • Apple and Google app stores handle downloads, and Apple's TestFlight handles test versions. They have their own privacy policies. If you've agreed to share crash reports or app usage with developers on your phone, I may see that information through Apple or Google.

The app doesn't use analytics, advertising or tracking tools.

Children

Mum Brain is made for parents and other adults. It isn't aimed at children, and I don't knowingly collect information from children.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete any information I hold about you, such as feedback you've sent or a shared family list. Contact me and I'll reply within a month. If you're unhappy with how I've handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Changes to this policy

If the app starts handling information differently, I'll update this page and change the date at the top. Bigger changes will also be mentioned in the app's update notes.

Help

  • Lost jobs? Go to Settings › Backup › Restore a backup and choose your latest backup file.
  • Alarms not arriving? Check that notifications are on in your phone's Settings › Notifications › Mum Brain. Alarms make no sound when your phone is on silent.
  • Shared list not updating? Make sure you're online, then close and reopen the app.
